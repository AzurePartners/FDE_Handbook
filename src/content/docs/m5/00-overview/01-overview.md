title: "Module 5: FDE Discovery, Consulting & Delivery"
---
**In one sentence:** Module 5 covers the non-code half of the FDE job, understanding an ambiguous problem, scoping it, designing the simplest solution, delivering it with the customer, and handing it off, which is what separates a Forward Deployed Engineer from an engineer who only ships code.

## What this module is

One knowledge point per page, each tagged with its **Row ID** (e.g. `M5-L4.2`) mapping to the master syllabus. It follows the full delivery lifecycle end to end. If a page and the syllabus disagree, the syllabus is the source of truth and gets updated first.
