**In one sentence:** Module 4 covers what it takes to connect an AI system to a customer's real tools and data, secure it, and run it reliably in production, the enterprise-grade skills that turn a working prototype into something a business can depend on.

## What this module is

This module follows the syllabus one knowledge point per page. Each page carries its **Row ID** (e.g. `M4-L2.1`) so it maps directly to the master syllabus row; if a page and the syllabus ever disagree, the syllabus wins and gets updated first. Pages are written for two readers: a beginner using the sidebar to "see the map," and someone reviewing everything before an interview.

## The lessons

| Lesson | Focus | Rows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1\. External APIs, MCP & Connector Design | Exposing and consuming tools/APIs cleanly | 4 |
| 2\. Authentication, Authorization, RBAC & Secrets | Proving identity and controlling access | 5 |
| 3\. Real-World Data | Quality, freshness, provenance, entity resolution, contracts, compliance | 6 |
| 4\. Reliability, Cost, Latency & Scale | Keeping production fast, cheap, and up | 4 |
| 5\. Deployment, CI/CD, Observability & Production Readiness | Shipping and operating the system | 4 |

Some adjacent topics (prompt injection, guardrails, evaluation, human-in-the-loop as a safety technique) live in Modules 2–3; where they matter here, pages link to them rather than repeat them.
